Respite and Short Breaks

A proper break for parents and carers, while the person you care for spends time with someone they trust.

Practical help for tenants in supported living, working alongside their registered care provider.

  • Bills, tenancy letters and repairs

Supervised Contact

Safe, well-recorded family time between children and their families, arranged by the local authority.

  • Supervised and supported contact sessions
  • Collecting and returning children

Welfare Checks

Visits to make sure a child, young person or adult is safe and well, with a clear report back the same day.

  • Planned or urgent visits

  • Concerns escalated straight away under safeguarding procedures

Youth Escorting

Safe, reliable escorts for children and young people travelling to school, family time, appointments and placements.

  • Trained, enhanced DBS-checked escorts
  • On foot, by public transport or by taxi
  • Journeys to school, contact, court and appointments
  • A handover note at every drop-off

Befriending

Regular, friendly company for people who are lonely or isolated, matched with someone they get on with.

  • Walks, cafés, libraries and community groups

  • Help reconnecting with local clubs and faith groups

Postnatal and Doula Support

Calm, practical help for new parents in the weeks after birth. Non-clinical, working alongside your midwife and health visitor.

  • Meals, laundry and household help
  • Looking after older siblings and school runs
  • Rest and recovery time for parents
  • Birth doula support coming soon

What we don't do

Familine Care is a non-clinical service. To keep everyone safe, our team never provides:

  • Personal care (washing, dressing, toileting)
  • Administering medication
  • Lifting or moving people
  • Overnight accommodation
  • Clinical or medical advice
  • Legal advice

If someone needs personal care, we'll help you find a registered care provider and keep supporting with everything else.

For councils, schools, courts and police

Specialist services for commissioners

Assessments, conferences and round-the-clock cover for children's and adult services, schools and academy trusts, courts, youth justice teams and police custody suites. Assessments are written only by Social Work England-registered social workers.

Independent Social Work Assessments

Assessments written by experienced, Social Work England-registered independent social workers, delivered on time and ready for court.

  • Special guardianship (SGO) assessments
  • Viability and connected-person assessments
  • Parenting assessments
  • Reports for court and pre-proceedings
  • Fixed fee, quoted per assessment

Family Group Conferences

Independent coordinators who bring the family network together to make a safe plan for the child, helping councils meet the duty to offer family group decision-making before care proceedings.

  • Preparation with every family member
  • Held in the family’s language and community
  • Private family time and a written family plan
  • Review conferences

Return Home Interviews

Independent conversations with children after they’ve been missing, to understand why they went and what would keep them safe.

  • Offered within 72 hours of the child’s return
  • Independent of the police and the child’s social worker
  • Exploitation and safeguarding concerns shared straight away
  • A written report with clear recommendations

School Attendance Outreach

Practical, supportive outreach for schools, academy trusts and councils, helping children back into school and keeping them there.

  • First-day absence home visits and welfare checks
  • Morning support to get children up, ready and into school
  • Escorting and phased returns for anxious children
  • Attendance plans and meetings with the family and school
  • Links to Early Help, mental health and SEND support
  • A short written update to the school after every visit

SGO and Kinship Support

Practical help for grandparents, relatives and friends raising a child under a special guardianship order or kinship arrangement.

  • Supervised and supported family time with birth parents
  • Help with routines, school and paperwork
  • Respite breaks for kinship carers
  • Life story work alongside the child’s social worker
  • Peer support groups for special guardians

Exploitation Mentoring

One-to-one mentoring for young people at risk of county lines, criminal or sexual exploitation.

  • A consistent, trusted mentor
  • Goals for education, training and positive activities
  • Works hand in hand with return home interviews
  • Regular updates to the lead professional

Child Protection Minute-Taking

Trained, accurate minute-takers for child protection conferences, core group meetings and strategy meetings.

  • Clear, impartial minutes
  • Delivered within agreed timescales
  • Secure handling of sensitive information
  • In person or online

Court Support Worker

Practical and emotional support for parents, young people and families going to court.

  • Going to court with them on the day
  • Explaining what will happen and when
  • Help with paperwork, travel and childcare
  • Working alongside their solicitor, never instead of one

Adult Out of Hours

Registered social workers and experienced support staff to support your adult emergency duty team when demand peaks.

  • Evening, overnight and weekend visits
  • Welfare and safeguarding checks
  • Support while statutory services respond to a crisis
  • Written records by the next morning

Children Out of Hours

Social Work England-registered social workers and support staff for children’s emergency duty teams.

  • Out-of-hours visits and welfare checks
  • Support for placements at risk of breaking down
  • Escorting during emergency placement moves
  • Handover records by the next morning

Appropriate Adult Service

Trained appropriate adults for children and vulnerable adults detained or interviewed by police, as required under PACE.

  • Attendance at police custody, day and night
  • Making sure the person understands their rights
  • Supporting communication during interviews
  • Making sure they can speak to a solicitor

Familine Care does not give legal advice or represent anyone in court, and attendance enforcement such as fines and prosecutions stays with the school and council. Anyone who is arrested has the right to free legal advice from a duty solicitor, and we make sure they are put in touch with one.
